Say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what you were doing when something went wrong. A screenshot or the file that caused trouble helps most.

Quick answers

  • Where are my originals? Untouched in Photos, Files or wherever they came from. The app works on its own copies and only ever adds new items to your library.
  • The app can't see my photo library. That's by design. It asks only for permission to add. Bring pictures in through the photo picker, Files, the clipboard, drag and drop, or another app's share sheet.
  • A video says it's coming soon. ProRes RAW and Apple Log 2 videos aren't supported yet. Every other photo and video format, including RAW, ProRAW, HDR and Apple Log, develops today.
  • My edits disappeared after an update. Open Settings > Library Check in the app. It lists anything it couldn't read and offers a fix for each.
  • Exports stop when I leave the app. Keep the app open while a long video exports. The export says up front whether it can continue in the background.
Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no uploads, no tracking.
