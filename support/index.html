Say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what you were doing when something went wrong. A screenshot or the file that caused trouble helps most.

Quick answers

  • Where are my originals? In Photos, as they were. A photo's edit is saved into the photo the way Photos' own edits are, so in the Photos app, Edit > Revert brings the original back. Videos and collages are saved as new copies beside their originals.
  • The app can't see my photo library. The Library tab needs access to Photos. Open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Darkprint and choose Full Access, or Limited Access to pick the photos it can see. With Limited Access, Select More Photos in the Library adds more.
  • Where did my edit go? A photo's saved edit is in the photo itself: open the photo in Darkprint's Library and the edit comes back to change. Drafts, edits not saved yet, wait in the Darkroom tab with a pencil mark.
  • A video says it's coming soon. ProRes RAW and Apple Log 2 videos aren't supported yet. Every other photo and video format, including RAW, ProRAW, HDR and Apple Log, develops today.
  • A photo says its edit was made with a newer version. Update Darkprint from the App Store to change that edit. Photos keeps it as it is until you do.
  • Exports stop when I leave the app. Keep the app open while a long video exports. The export says up front whether it can continue in the background.
Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no uploads, no tracking.
