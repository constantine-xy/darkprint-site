Darkprint

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Darkprint develops photos and videos on your iPhone. It has no account, sends nothing anywhere, and collects no data about you.

Your photos and videos

  • Everything happens on your iPhone. Developing, previews and exports run on the device. Nothing is uploaded or processed on a server.
  • Importing makes a copy. The app keeps its own copy of each photo or video you bring in, in its private storage. Your originals are never modified or deleted.
  • Saving adds a new copy to Photos. A developed photo or video goes into your library as a new item.
  • Deleting the app deletes its copies and edits. The app's storage is excluded from iCloud Backup, because every file in it is a copy of something you keep elsewhere.

Photos access

  • By default the app asks only for permission to add to your photo library. With that permission it cannot read your library at all.
  • If you turn on the Darkprint album in Settings, the app asks for Full Access, which iOS requires to create and find an album. It uses that access only to find its own album, and never reads, changes or deletes anything else.
  • You can change either permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Metadata

A photo or video you import may carry metadata such as the camera's date, the exposure and a location. The app keeps that metadata in its copy, reads it for features like the date stamp and the camera bar, and writes it into a developed copy only as your export options say. Location is included only when you choose Include Location.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising and no third-party code.
  • No network connection. The app never contacts the internet.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
